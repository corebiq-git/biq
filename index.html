Implementation notes

This is a private front-end specification only. It does not submit, store or transmit real client data. Credential/password fields must be encrypted and access-controlled in production.

RuleMeaning
*Mandatory field. Form cannot be finalized without a valid value.
#Auto-generated/system field. Normally read-only.
~Optional field unless a conditional rule makes it mandatory.
@Format/validation rule. Examples: PAN, GSTIN, IFSC, mobile, PIN code, email, URL.
